Case Study 01 · Forecasting + MLOps

Northlight Capital — models that rebuilt trust

The Challenge

Northlight had grown through acquisition, and its data showed it — a patchwork of spreadsheets, siloed systems, and guess-driven forecasts. Analysts loved the domain; the infrastructure kept getting in the way. We were asked to unify the data story and ship a production forecasting platform in one quarter.

The Approach

We rebuilt Northlight around a single promise — "Clarity for complex capital." A unified feature store, MLOps pipelines, and a monitoring stack feeding a forecasting model that analysts could trace back to the raw data behind every number.

Case Study 02 · Forecasting + Data

Mono Coffee Co. — demand forecasting for a craft brand

The Challenge

A single-origin roaster with beautiful beans and spreadsheet forecasts — constantly out of stock on bestsellers and over-rotating slow movers. Mono needed demand planning that felt as considered as its roasting, without the guesswork.

The Approach

We trained a forecasting model on POS, subscription, and promotional data: an ensemble that predicts daily demand at SKU level, balances stock and batch freshness, and feeds ordering recommendations back into their workflow.
